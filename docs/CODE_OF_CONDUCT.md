# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the Encre project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community
- Recognizing that Encre is a multi-language project (Python, Rust, TypeScript) and that not every contributor is fluent in every layer

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Dismissing or attacking accessibility requests, language preferences, or skill levels
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all Encre community spaces — both project spaces and public spaces when an individual is officially representing the project. Concretely, that includes:

- The **encre/encre** GitHub repository and its issues, pull requests, discussions, and wiki
- The **dunimd/encre** Gitee mirror and its issues
- The official communication channels listed in [CONTRIBUTING.md → Communication Channels](CONTRIBUTING.md#communication-channels) (GitHub Discussions, X / Twitter, Bilibili, maintainer email)
- Public events (conferences, meetups, livestreams) where someone is representing Encre as a maintainer or designated contributor
- Social media interactions where you are speaking in an Encre capacity (e.g., replying from the `@Dunimd2025` account or using an `encre` hashtag)

The CoC does **not** govern private conversations between individual contributors, nor the content of third-party forks.

## Your Rights as a Community Member

The CoC is not only about what you must not do. As a member of the Encre community you can expect:

- **To be credited** for your work. Substantive contributions are listed in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) and in [THANKS.md](THANKS.md) — see [CONTRIBUTING.md → Your Rights as a Contributor](CONTRIBUTING.md#your-rights-as-a-contributor) for the full deal.
- **To be answered.** Open issues and PRs receive a response (even if it's "we can't get to this right now") within the SLAs in [CONTRIBUTING.md → Pull Request Process](CONTRIBUTING.md#pull-request-process). Silence is a bug; please ping if you hit it.
- **To disagree.** Disagreements about technical direction belong in issues or discussions, not in personal attacks. Maintainers will explain the reasoning behind a decision and update it if persuaded.
- **To be anonymous** when reporting a CoC violation, if you wish. See [Reporting Process](#reporting-process) below.
- **To challenge a moderation decision.** See [Appeals](#appeals).
- **To be protected by the CoC yourself.** The same standards apply to maintainers. If a maintainer violates the CoC, report to the address below; reports about maintainers are reviewed by other maintainers.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Reporting Process

### How to Report

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainers in private:

🌐 **conduct.dunimd.com** *(pending activation)*

Use this address for **CoC violations only**. For general project questions, use [Gitee Issues](https://gitee.com/dunimd/encre/issues) or [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/mf2023/Encre/issues). For **security vulnerabilities**, use the address in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead — never post security issues publicly.

For sensitive cases, encrypt to GPG `54B6 1520 0435 612E E91C F180 24AD F10D D207 AA1F` (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md#contact)).

### What to Include

A useful report contains:

- **Your contact information** (so we can follow up; tell us if you want to stay anonymous)
- **Who was involved** — real names, usernames, or pseudonyms; links to issues / comments / commits / chat excerpts
- **What happened** — a factual, chronological description. Quote messages verbatim when possible.
- **When it happened** — dates, times, time zones if relevant
- **Where it happened** — GitHub issue, PR, discussion, chat platform, in-person event
- **What you'd like to happen** — public apology, content removal, ban, mediation, nothing (you may simply be informing us)
- **Anything else** — screenshots, logs, links to related conversations

### Confidentiality

All reports are handled with discretion. We will:

- Keep your identity confidential unless you explicitly allow us to share it or we are legally required to disclose it
- Share the report only with maintainers who need to know to investigate and respond
- Never retaliate against anyone who reports a violation in good faith

If a conflict of interest exists between a maintainer and a reporter (same employer, prior dispute, etc.), that maintainer will be excluded from the review.

### Response Time

We aim to:

- **Acknowledge** receipt within **24 hours** of your report
- **Provide a substantive response** within **5 business days**
- **Resolve** the situation as quickly as the facts allow; complex cases may take longer, and we will keep you informed

If we miss the 24-hour acknowledgment window, please follow up — email sometimes gets filtered as spam, and we'd rather hear from you twice than not at all.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will follow these Community Impact Guidelines when determining consequences for an action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct. The ladder is intentionally graduated; most situations are resolved at the earliest step.

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact:** Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome in the community.

**Consequence:** A private, written warning from project maintainers, providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact:** A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence:** A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a specified period of time. This includes avoiding interactions in community spaces as well as external channels. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact:** A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period of time. No public or private interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, is allowed during this period. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact:** A pattern of violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals.

**Consequence:** A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the community.

## What Happens After a Decision

For every confirmed violation we publish a short, anonymized summary in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) under a "Community" section. The summary records what rule was broken, which level of the ladder was applied, and the duration. Names of the reporter are **never** published; names of the person sanctioned are not published unless they consent.

This serves three purposes:

- The community can see that the CoC is enforced, not just a wall decoration
- The ladder is applied consistently, not ad hoc
- Future maintainers can see precedent

## Appeals

If you believe you have been falsely or unfairly accused of violating this Code of Conduct, submit a concise description of your grievance at **conduct.dunimd.com** *(pending activation)*. Appeals are reviewed by maintainers who were not involved in the original decision. The decision of the appeal panel is final within the project's governance.

## Community Values

The standards above are the floor. We also encourage community members to embrace these values in day-to-day work:

### Collaboration

- Work together toward common goals
- Share knowledge and help others learn — especially across language boundaries (Python ↔ Rust ↔ TypeScript)
- Give credit where credit is due
- Be patient with newcomers; remember you were one once

### Excellence

- Strive for high-quality contributions
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully
- Continuously improve and learn
- Help maintain code quality and documentation

### Transparency

- Communicate openly about decisions, including the *why*
- Provide clear reasoning for changes; link to the discussion that produced them
- Admit mistakes and learn from them publicly
- Keep the community informed of roadmap shifts

### Respect

- Value diverse perspectives and experiences — many of our contributors come from very different ecosystems (chat platforms, IDEs, ML frameworks)
- Treat all contributors with dignity
- Listen actively and considerately
- Assume good intentions, and ask before you assume the worst

## Updates to This Code

This Code of Conduct may be updated from time to time. When we change it, we will:

- Post an announcement in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/mf2023/Encre/discussions) and on the [Gitee mirror](https://gitee.com/dunimd/encre)
- Bump the "Last Updated" date below
- Note significant changes in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) under a "Community" heading
- Where the change is material (e.g. a new protected class), give **30 days' notice** before the new version takes effect
