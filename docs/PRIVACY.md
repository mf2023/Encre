## 1. Introduction

Dunimd ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of all users of our desktop application and associated services (collectively, the "Software"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Software.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the Software, you consent to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not use the Software.

**Contact:** dunimd@outlook.com

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

We collect information you voluntarily provide when using the Software, including:

- **Account Configuration**: Backend service endpoints, API keys, model preferences, and custom settings you configure within the Software.
- **User Content**: Messages, prompts, files, and other content you submit through the Software for processing by AI models.
- **Communication Data**: Information you provide when contacting our support team or submitting feedback.
- **Authentication Data**: Credentials or tokens used to authenticate with third-party AI model providers.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the Software, we may automatically collect:

- **Usage Data**: Aggregated statistics about feature usage, session duration, and interaction patterns. This data is anonymized where possible and used solely for product improvement.
- **Diagnostic Data**: Error logs, crash reports, and performance metrics that help us identify and resolve technical issues. You can control the collection of diagnostic data through the Software's settings.
- **Device Information**: Operating system type and version, application version, and hardware identifiers (collected only for compatibility and troubleshooting purposes).
- **Network Information**: IP address and network connectivity status (collected for security and connectivity purposes).

### 2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

We want to be transparent about what we do not collect:

- We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or physical address unless you voluntarily provide it.
- We do not collect browsing history, bookmarks, or files outside of those you explicitly choose to use with the Software.
- We do not collect keystroke data, mouse movements, or other behavioral biometrics.
- We do not collect location data beyond coarse geolocation derived from your IP address for security purposes.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- **Service Operation**: To process your requests, execute AI model interactions, and deliver the functionality of the Software.
- **Improvement and Development**: To analyze usage patterns, diagnose technical issues, and enhance the performance, security, and user experience of the Software.
- **Security**: To detect, prevent, and respond to potential security incidents, unauthorized access, or violations of our Terms of Service.
- **Compliance**: To comply with applicable legal obligations, regulatory requirements, and lawful requests from public authorities.
- **Customer Support**: To provide technical assistance and respond to your inquiries.

### 3.1 AI Model Processing

When you submit content to the Software for processing by AI models:

- Your content is transmitted to the AI model backend you have configured (which may be a local model, a third-party API, or your own infrastructure).
- We do not use your content to train or improve AI models unless you explicitly opt in.
- You retain full ownership of your content. We claim no intellectual property rights over the content you submit.
- Content transmitted to third-party AI providers is subject to their respective privacy policies and terms of service.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy:

- **User Content**: Retained locally on your device and as long as needed for active sessions. You may delete your content at any time through the Software interface. We do not retain your content on our servers.
- **Usage and Diagnostic Data**: Retained for a period of up to 12 months, after which it is anonymized or deleted.
- **Configuration Data**: Retained until you modify or delete your settings.
- **Communication Records**: Retained for up to 24 months for customer support purposes, unless deletion is requested earlier.

You can delete all local data at any time by clearing application data through the Software settings or your operating system.

## 7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information:

- **Encryption in Transit**: All data transmitted between the Software and configured backends is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher.
- **Encryption at Rest**: Sensitive data stored locally (including API keys) is encrypted using AES-256 encryption.
- **Access Controls**: We follow the principle of least access for any staff who may need to access user data for support or maintenance purposes.
- **Regular Audits**: Our security practices are regularly reviewed and updated to address emerging threats.
- **Vulnerability Management**: We accept responsible disclosure of security vulnerabilities.

### 7.1 Data Localization

The Software is designed to operate with your choice of backend. You have full control over where your data is processed:

- **Local Mode**: All processing occurs on your device. No data leaves your machine.
- **Self-Hosted Backend**: You may configure the Software to connect to your own infrastructure, giving you complete control over data location and handling.
- **Third-Party Backend**: If you choose to use a third-party AI provider, their privacy policy will also apply to the data you submit to them.
