## 1. Who We Are and Legal Framework

1.1 The controller is **Dunimd**. Contact: dunimd@outlook.com · dunimd.com · GitHub: github.com/mf2023/Encre. Services include Encre Agent (AI agent platform), PiscesLx (hosted LLM inference), StadionOS, and Dunimd Enterprise/Cloud/Studio/Support.

1.2 Applicable framework:

| Instrument | Relevance |
|---|---|
| Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) + 13 Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) | core data protection law |
| Notifiable Data Breaches scheme (Part IIIC) | breach assessment/notification |
| Privacy and Other Legislation Amendment Act 2024 | statutory privacy tort; ADM transparency phased; Children's Online Privacy Code in development |
| Spam Act 2003 | marketing opt-in |
| Online Safety Act 2021 | content-safety duties (see Content Guidelines) |

## 2. What We Collect (Local-First)

2.1 **On-device by design：** agent definitions, prompts, responses, conversation history, and integration credentials remain on your device. Telemetry is off by default; prompts and responses are not stored server-side by default.

2.2 Only when you enable optional cloud services do we process：

| Category | Examples | Retention |
|---|---|---|
| Account data | email address | until deletion |
| Service metadata | timestamps, error codes | max. 30 days |
| Telemetry (opt-in) | diagnostics | max. 90 days |
| Inference requests (PiscesLx) | processed transiently | deleted after processing |

## 3. Purposes and No-Training Rule

3.1 Purposes: providing and securing the services, support, optional cloud features, legal compliance.

3.2 **We do not train models on your content without your express consent.** Local content is never uploaded for training purposes.

## 4. Disclosure and Overseas Transfer

4.1 We do not sell personal information.

4.2 Where disclosure to processors is necessary, we bind them contractually.

4.3 For overseas disclosures (APP 8), we take reasonable steps to ensure recipients do not breach the APPs — typically via contractual safeguards consistent with recognised international standards.

## 5. Your Rights

5.1 Under APP 12 and APP 13 you may request access to and correction of your personal information.

5.2 You may also object to processing, withdraw consent, or request deletion; we respond within a reasonable period (customarily around 30 days).

5.3 Complaints: contact us first at dunimd@outlook.com. You may escalate to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC), oaic.gov.au, tel 1300 363 992.

## 6. Children and Young People

6.1 Our services are not directed at children under 13. Users under 18 require parental/guardian consent as a prudent standard.

6.2 A dedicated Children's Online Privacy Code is under development (due around December 2026); we will align when finalised. See [Minors' Privacy](MINORS_PRIVACY.md).

## 7. Security and Data Breaches (NDB Scheme)

7.1 Measures: TLS 1.2+ in transit, AES-256-GCM at rest, least-privilege access.

7.2 Under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme we assess suspected breaches within 30 days; where an eligible data breach is likely to result in serious harm, we notify the OAIC and affected individuals as soon as practicable.

## 8. Artificial Intelligence

8.1 There is no comprehensive Australian AI statute; we apply voluntary transparency measures aligned with emerging federal guidance.

8.2 AI-generated outputs may be inaccurate; verify important results. Where you interact with AI systems, this is disclosed.

## 9. Changes and Contact

9.1 Material changes will be announced at least 30 days before taking effect.

9.2 Contact: dunimd@outlook.com · dunimd.com · Regulator: OAIC, oaic.gov.au
