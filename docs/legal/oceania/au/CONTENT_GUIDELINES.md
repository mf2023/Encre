## 1. Purpose and Architecture

1.1 Encre Agent is an open platform: agents and prompts are processed locally on your device by default.

1.2 These guidelines apply once content is shared, published, or optional services are used.

| Layer | Processing | Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Local on-device agents | no server-side checks | your responsibility |
| Shared/published agents | moderation | fully applies |
| Cloud inference (PiscesLx) | technical processing | abuse prevention |
| Chat platform integrations | platform rules also apply | follow platform policies |

## 2. Prohibited Content

Prohibited via shared or cloud services：

- **child exploitation material** — Criminal Code Act 1995 offences; we escalate to ACCCE and law enforcement immediately;
- **non-consensual intimate images** — removal notices under the Online Safety Act 2021 are respected; eSafety Commissioner determinations complied with;
- class 1 material within the meaning of the Online Safety Act 2021 (terrorism, extreme violence)；
- doxxing content (Criminal Code doxxing offences introduced in 2024), threats, harassment, and incitement;
- defamatory material targeting identifiable individuals;
- copyright infringement (Copyright Act 1968);
- malware, phishing, and fraud schemes;
- other unlawful content.

## 3. AI-Specific Rules

3.1 **Transparency：** AI-generated content must not be passed off as human-created; synthetic media ("deepfakes") must be labelled as such.

3.2 **No deception：** using AI to defraud, deceive, or harm reputations is prohibited.

3.3 **Responsibility：** publishers of AI outputs are responsible for them.

## 4. Reporting and Enforcement

4.1 Report violations to **dunimd@outlook.com** with context. Child-safety reports trigger immediate escalation to ACCCE/esafety.gov.au channels.

4.2 Enforcement ladder: warning → feature restriction → content removal → account ban; each measure may be appealed by reply email.

4.3 We comply with eSafety removal notices and Basic Online Safety Expectations where they apply.

## 5. Changes

Changes will be announced at least 30 days in advance.
