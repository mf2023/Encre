## 1. Principles

1.1 We process children's personal information minimally and only for necessary purposes.

1.2 No advertising targeting of children; no use of children's data for marketing.

1.3 Our services are general-purpose and not directed at children.

## 2. Age Thresholds and Parental Consent

2.1 Cloud-service registration requires a minimum age of **13 years**. Users under 18 require parental or guardian consent as our prudent standard, pending the Children's Online Privacy Code (in development, expected around December 2026).

2.2 Local core functions need no account; the same age thresholds apply by analogy.

2.3 If we learn that we hold a child's data without required consent, we delete it promptly.

## 3. Parents' Rights

3.1 Parents and guardians may exercise all rights under the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) §5 on a child's behalf, including access, correction, deletion, and objection.

3.2 Requests to dunimd@outlook.com — answered within around **30 days**.

## 4. Local-First Architecture as a Safeguard

4.1 Encre Agent stores everything locally by default: prompts, responses, and history never leave your device.

4.2 Telemetry is off by default; prompts and responses are not stored server-side by default.

4.3 Children's content is never used for model training without express consent.

## 5. School and Non-Commercial Use

5.1 Schools may deploy Encre Agent for teaching; data-minimising settings at class level are recommended.

5.2 Schools remain responsible for their own privacy obligations and parental communications.

## 6. Contact and Reporting

Questions, parent requests, and reports: dunimd@outlook.com · dunimd.com

Regulator: Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC), oaic.gov.au · Child-safety escalation: ACCCE (accce.gov.au) and the eSafety Commissioner (esafety.gov.au)
