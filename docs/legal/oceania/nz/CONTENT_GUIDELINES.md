## 1. Purpose and Architecture

1.1 Encre Agent processes agents and prompts locally by default; these guidelines apply when you publish, share or connect content to optional services.

| Layer | Handling | Guidelines |
|---|---|---|
| Local agent on device | no server inspection | your responsibility |
| Shared/published agents | moderated | fully applicable |
| Cloud inference (PiscesLx) | technical processing | abuse prevention |
| Chat-platform integrations | platform rules parallel | follow those policies too |

## 2. Prohibited Content

Prohibited via shared or cloud surfaces:

- **Objectionable material**, including child sexual exploitation material, within the meaning of the Films, Videos, and Publications Classification Act 1993 — immediate escalation to the Department of Internal Affairs;
- content that contravenes the Harmful Digital Communications Act 2015 communication principles: harassment, intimidation, incitement to self-harm, denigration, disclosure of sensitive personal facts or intimate images without consent;
- credible threats of violence, stalking or doxxing;
- fraud, phishing and malware distribution (Crimes Act 1961 ss. 249–252);
- infringement of copyright (Copyright Act 1994) or other intellectual property;
- other content prohibited by New Zealand law.

## 3. AI-Specific Rules

3.1 AI-generated media must be identifiable as synthetic where a reasonable person could be misled; undisclosed deepfakes targeting real people are prohibited.

3.2 Impersonation of individuals or of Dunimd is not allowed.

3.3 Users who publish AI output are responsible for it.

## 4. Reporting and Enforcement

4.1 Report violations to **dunimd@outlook.com** with the relevant content and context. CSAM reports trigger immediate escalation to authorities.

4.2 HDCA context: Netsafe operates as the approved agency for digital communication complaints; we cooperate with its notice process and maintain takedown procedures consistent with the safe harbour provisions.

4.3 Escalating measures: warning → feature restriction → removal → suspension. Every measure can be appealed by replying to the enforcement notice.

## 5. Changes

Changes to these guidelines are published at least 30 days before taking effect.
