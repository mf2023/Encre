## 1. Principles

1.1 The Privacy Act 2020 applies to children's information with the same force as adults'; we treat it as inherently more sensitive.

1.2 We do not profile minors for advertising or sell their information.

## 2. Age Limits and Parental Consent

2.1 Cloud accounts require a minimum age of 13. If you are under 18 we ask for verifiable parental or guardian consent before activation.

2.2 Local basic functions require no account; the same age expectations apply.

2.3 If we learn that a minor's information is held without the required consent, we delete it promptly.

## 3. Parents' Rights

3.1 Parents and guardians may exercise the rights described in the [Privacy Statement](PRIVACY.md) section 6 on a child's behalf:

- access to the child's information;
- correction and deletion requests;
- withdrawal of consent.

3.2 Requests go to dunimd@outlook.com; we respond within approximately 20 working days.

## 4. Local-First Architecture as Protection

4.1 Prompts, responses and history stay on the device by default and are never stored server-side unless a cloud feature is switched on.

4.2 Telemetry is off by default; nothing about a minor's usage leaves the machine without explicit opt-in.

4.3 We never use minors' content for model training without express consent.

## 5. Schools and Non-Commercial Use

5.1 Schools may deploy Encre Agent for teaching. We act as a processor and handle student data solely on documented school instructions.

5.2 Schools remain responsible for informing parents consistent with their own privacy obligations under the Privacy Act 2020 and NZCER/board policies.

## 6. Contact and Reporting

6.1 Questions, parental requests or reports: dunimd@outlook.com · dunimd.com

6.2 Harmful online behaviour affecting young people can also be reported to Netsafe (netsafe.org.nz). Content that may be objectionable should be referred to the Department of Internal Affairs.
