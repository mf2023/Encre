## 2. Prohibited content

Content contrary to UAE law, notably Federal Decree-Law No. 34 of 2021 (Combating Rumours and Cybercrimes) and Federal Decree-Law No. 31 of 2021 (Penal Code), is prohibited, including:

- Child sexual abuse material — zero tolerance; immediate escalation to authorities;
- Content ridiculing or insulting religions or inciting sectarian strife;
- Rumours, fabricated news, or false information harming public order or national unity;
- Defamation, insults, and invasion of privacy (including publishing personal data or images of others without consent);
- Terrorist content or promotion of violence;
- Intellectual-property infringement (Federal Decree-Law No. 38 of 2021 on Copyright);
- Gambling, fraud, phishing, and deceptive schemes;
- Malware and unauthorized system access.

## 3. AI-specific rules

3.1 AI-generated content must be identifiable as such; deepfakes and synthetic media must be labelled.

3.2 Impersonating real persons via AI is prohibited.

3.3 Publishers of AI output are responsible for it.

## 4. Reporting and enforcement

4.1 Report violations to **dunimd@outlook.com** with content and context. CSAM reports are escalated immediately to law enforcement.

4.2 Enforcement ladder: warning → feature restriction → content removal → account termination. Every measure can be appealed.

4.3 We preserve evidence and cooperate with competent UAE authorities as required by law.

## 5. Changes

Material changes will be announced at least 30 days in advance.
