## 2. Legal framework

2.1 **Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL)**, in force since January 2022, is the principal federal data protection statute applicable to our processing of personal data of individuals located in the UAE.

2.2 Supporting federal statutes include Federal Decree-Law No. 34 of 2021 on Combating Rumours and Cybercrimes and Federal Law No. 3 of 2016 (Child Rights Law, "Wadeema").

2.3 Financial free zones maintain their own data protection regimes (e.g., DIFC Data Protection Law No. 5 of 2020 and ADGM Data Protection Regulations 2021). Users located in those zones may additionally benefit from those regimes; this notice sets our baseline commitments.

2.4 We monitor guidance issued by the competent supervisory authorities, including the UAE Data Office, as oversight structures continue to develop, and we update our practices accordingly.

## 3. Data we collect

3.1 Encre Agent is designed local-first: agent definitions, prompts, responses, chat history, and integrations are stored **on your device** by default.

3.2 Telemetry is off by default. Prompts and responses are not stored server-side by default.

3.3 Cloud services (e.g., PiscesLx inference or account management) process only what is necessary to deliver them: account identifiers, service metadata (timestamps, error codes), and payment records for paid plans.

3.4 We do not collect special-category or sensitive personal data through ordinary use, and we do not knowingly collect children's data without verifiable parental consent ([MINORS_PRIVACY.md](MINORS_PRIVACY.md)).

## 4. Purposes and legal basis

We process personal data only where a legal basis applies under the PDPL, principally:

- **Your consent** (withdrawable at any time);
- Performance of a contract with you;
- Compliance with legal obligations;
- Our legitimate interests in securing and improving the services, balanced against your rights.

We do **not** use your prompts, responses, or content to train models without your separate, explicit consent.

## 5. Sharing and international transfers

5.1 We do not sell personal data. Sharing is limited to service providers bound by confidentiality and processing terms, and to authorities where legally compelled.

5.2 Where processing occurs outside the UAE, we rely on appropriate safeguards recognized under the PDPL framework (contractual clauses and comparable protections), consistent with our global transfer standards described in [DATA_PROCESSING.md](DATA_PROCESSING.md).

## 6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request:

1. Access to your personal data and information about its processing;
2. Correction of inaccurate data;
3. Deletion of data no longer needed;
4. Restriction or objection to certain processing;
5. Withdrawal of consent without affecting prior lawful processing.

Send requests to dunimd@outlook.com. We respond within approximately 30 days, extendable where complexity requires.

## 7. Security and breach response

7.1 Technical measures include transport encryption (TLS 1.2+) and encryption at rest (AES-256-GCM); organizational measures include access controls and staff confidentiality duties.

7.2 If a personal data breach is likely to cause risk to you, we will notify the competent authority and affected individuals without undue delay, in line with PDPL requirements once implementing processes are fully operational.

## 8. Artificial intelligence

8.1 Our AI features are transparent: outputs are machine-generated and may be inaccurate; important decisions should not rest solely on AI output.

8.2 We do not deploy systems for social scoring, unlawful surveillance, or other purposes prohibited under UAE cybercrime and public-order law.

8.3 Synthetic media must be labelled; impersonation is prohibited under the [User Agreement](USER_AGREEMENT.md).
