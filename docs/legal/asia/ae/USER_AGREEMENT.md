## 4. Prohibited use

Prohibited conduct includes conduct contrary to Federal Decree-Law No. 34 of 2021 on Combating Rumours and Cybercrimes, including:

- Unauthorized access to systems or interference with their operation;
- Fraud, phishing, and deceptive schemes;
- Dissemination of rumours, false news, or content inciting hatred;
- Impersonation of others or of Dunimd;
- Circumventing access controls or overloading the service.
