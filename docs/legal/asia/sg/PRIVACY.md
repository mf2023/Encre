**Your rights:** access, correction, withdrawal of consent — via [dunimd@outlook.com](mailto:dunimd@outlook.com) (response ≤30 days). Complaints: PDPC.

**Breach:** notification per PDPA Part 6 (PDPC + affected individuals where significant harm/≥500).
