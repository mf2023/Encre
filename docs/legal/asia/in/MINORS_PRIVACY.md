# Minors' Data Protection Rules (India)

**Last updated: 22 August 2026**
**Scope:** Users under 18 and their parents/guardians

> Parent documents: [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md), [User Agreement](USER_AGREEMENT.md)

## 1. Basis

DPDP Act 2023 **§9** — processing of children's data (under 18) requires **verifiable parental consent** and must not involve tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising directed at children (subject to Rule exemptions when notified); Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection) Act, 2015 principles.

## 2. Age Bands

| Age | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Under 18 | **Verifiable parental consent required** before any collection. Dunimd does not target this group. |
| Paid features | Guardian consent additionally advised under the Indian Majority Act framework. |

## 3. Principles

Minimal collection; no profiling/targeted ads on minors; no third-party disclosure without consent; deletion after purpose ends; enhanced security.

## 4. Guardian Rights

Guardians may exercise access/correction/erasure/stop-processing on a child's behalf via [Privacy](PRIVACY.md) §6 channels within DPDP Rules timelines.

## 5. Local-First Note

Encre local mode stores everything on-device only — nothing reaches us. OS-level parental controls recommended. Review any cloud provider's child policy before connecting.
