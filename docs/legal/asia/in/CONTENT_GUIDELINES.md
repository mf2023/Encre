# Content Guidelines (India)

**Last updated: 22 August 2026**

> Parents: [Terms](TERMS.md), [User Agreement](USER_AGREEMENT.md). Basis: IT Act 2000 §66–67 & §79, Indian Penal Code/Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita 2023 (defamation, obscenity, cyber offences), POCSO Act 2012 (zero tolerance), Copyright Act 1957, MeitY advisories on deepfakes and AI labelling.

## 1. Prohibited Content

1. Child sexual abuse material (**absolute ban**, reported to authorities);
2. Defamation or privacy invasion (incl. publishing identifying personal data without consent);
3. IP infringement;
4. Fraud, malware, hacking assistance;
5. Deepfake misinformation / election-interference content;
6. Non-consensual intimate imagery;
7. Content otherwise unlawful in India.

## 2. AI-Output Duties

Human verification for professional use; label AI-generated media when publishing (consistent with MeitY's AI labelling direction); obtain permissions for real-person outputs.

## 3. Responsibility by Architecture

| Setup | Where processed | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Encre local mode + local model | Your device | User |
| Encre + third-party cloud model | Device → provider directly | That provider + user |
| PiscesLx hosted inference | Dunimd infra | Dunimd |

**You remain directly liable for what you publish.**

## 4. Enforcement & Reporting

Warning → restriction → suspension/termination. Report: [dunimd@outlook.com](mailto:dunimd@outlook.com); cybercrime: cybercrime.gov.in / helpline 1930; response within 15 working days.
