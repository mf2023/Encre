## 2. Legal framework

2.1 The **Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL)**, issued by Royal Decree No. M/19 dated 16 September 2021 and effective since 14 September 2023, governs our processing of personal data of individuals in the Kingdom. Its implementing regulations were approved in 2023 and we apply them as published by the regulator.

2.2 The supervisory authority is the **Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA)**, which also issues national AI ethics guidance relevant to our services.

2.3 Supporting statutes include the Anti-Cyber Crime Law (Royal Decree No. M/17 of 2007) and the Child Protection Law of 2014.

## 3. Data we collect

3.1 Encre Agent is designed local-first: agent definitions, prompts, responses, chat history, and integrations are stored **on your device** by default.

3.2 Telemetry is off by default. Prompts and responses are not stored server-side by default.

3.3 Optional cloud services (e.g., PiscesLx inference or accounts) process only what is necessary: account identifiers, service metadata, and payment records.

3.4 We do not collect sensitive data through ordinary use and never collect children's data without verifiable parental consent ([MINORS_PRIVACY.md](MINORS_PRIVACY.md)).

## 4. Purposes and legal basis

Processing relies on the bases recognized under the PDPL, principally:

- **Your consent** (withdrawable at any time without affecting prior lawful processing);
- Performance of a contract with you;
- Compliance with legal obligations;
- Legitimate interests, where permitted, balanced against your rights and expectations.

Your prompts, responses, and content are **never** used to train models without your separate, explicit consent.

## 5. Sharing and international transfers

5.1 We do not sell personal data. Sharing is limited to service providers under contractual safeguards and to authorities where legally required.

5.2 Transfers of personal data outside the Kingdom follow the PDPL transfer framework: an adequate level of protection in the destination, appropriate safeguards (e.g., standard/contractual clauses), or a recognized exception. Details: [DATA_PROCESSING.md](DATA_PROCESSING.md).

## 6. Your rights

Subject to the PDPL, you may request:

1. Access to and a copy of your personal data (with reasonable exceptions);
2. Correction of inaccurate or incomplete data;
3. Deletion or destruction of data no longer needed;
4. Withdrawal of consent;
5. Information about the purposes and recipients of processing.

Send requests to dunimd@outlook.com. We respond within approximately 30 days, extendable where complexity requires, and we inform you if a request cannot be fulfilled under the law.

## 7. Security and breach response

7.1 Technical measures: transport encryption (TLS 1.2+), encryption at rest (AES-256-GCM), least-privilege access. Organizational measures: confidentiality undertakings and access logging.

7.2 Where a personal data breach is likely to cause harm, we notify **SDAIA within 72 hours** of becoming aware and inform affected individuals without undue delay.

## 8. Artificial intelligence

8.1 We align our AI features with SDAIA's AI Ethics Principles: transparency, fairness, and accountability. Outputs are machine-generated and may be inaccurate.

8.2 We do not deploy systems for social scoring, unlawful surveillance, or purposes contrary to public order under Saudi law.

8.3 Synthetic media must be labelled; impersonation is prohibited under the [User Agreement](USER_AGREEMENT.md).
