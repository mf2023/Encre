## 2. Prohibited content

Content contrary to Saudi law — notably the Anti-Cyber Crime Law (Royal Decree No. M/17 of 2007), the Copyright Law (Royal Decree No. M/41 of 2003 as amended), and public-order principles — is prohibited, including:

- Child sexual abuse material — zero tolerance; immediate escalation to authorities;
- Content infringing Islamic values, public order, or national security;
- Defamation, slander, and invasion of privacy via electronic means;
- Terrorist content or promotion of violence;
- Fraud, forgery, phishing, gambling, and deceptive schemes;
- Intellectual-property infringement;
- Malware and unauthorized access to systems.

## 3. AI-specific rules

3.1 AI-generated content must be identifiable as such; deepfakes and synthetic media must be labelled.

3.2 Impersonating real persons via AI is prohibited.

3.3 Publishers of AI output are responsible for it, consistent with SDAIA's AI Ethics Principles on accountability.

## 4. Reporting and enforcement

4.1 Report violations to **dunimd@outlook.com** with content and context. CSAM reports are escalated immediately to law enforcement.

4.2 Enforcement ladder: warning → feature restriction → content removal → account termination. Every measure can be appealed.

4.3 We preserve evidence and cooperate with competent Saudi authorities as required by law.

## 5. Changes

Material changes will be announced at least 30 days in advance.
