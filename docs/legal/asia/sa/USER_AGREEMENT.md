You may run agents locally, build workflows, connect optional cloud services, and use open-source components per their licences.

## 4. Prohibited use

Prohibited conduct includes conduct contrary to the Anti-Cyber Crime Law (Royal Decree No. M/17 of 2007), including:

- Unauthorized access to systems, or interception or disclosure of data without authorization;
- Defamation, slander, or invasion of privacy through electronic means;
- Fraud, forgery, and deceptive schemes;
- Content contrary to public order, religious values, or national security;
- Impersonation of others or of Dunimd;
- Circumventing access controls or overloading the service.
