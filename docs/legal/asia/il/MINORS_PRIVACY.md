## 1. Principles

We collect only minimal data from minors, run no behavioural advertising to children, and never use minors' content for model training without explicit parental consent.

## 2. Age thresholds and parental consent

2.1 Cloud accounts require a minimum age of 13; users under 18 require verifiable parental or guardian involvement consistent with Israeli guardianship law and prudent privacy practice.

2.2 Local basic functionality requires no account but remains subject to the same age guidance.

2.3 If we learn that we hold a minor's data without proper consent, we delete it promptly.

## 3. Parents' rights

Parents may exercise all rights in [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md) §6 on behalf of their child — access, correction, deletion — by writing to dunimd@outlook.com (approximately 30-day response).

## 4. Local-first protection by design

Encre Agent stores prompts, responses, and history on the device by default; telemetry is off by default; nothing leaves the device without your action. This architecture minimizes minors' data exposure from the outset.

## 5. Schools and educational use

Schools may deploy Encre Agent for teaching; class-level data minimization settings are recommended. Schools remain responsible for Ministry of Education requirements and their own policies.

## 6. Contact and reporting

Concerns about a minor's data or safety: dunimd@outlook.com · dunimd.com
