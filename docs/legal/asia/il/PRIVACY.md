## 2. Legal framework

2.1 The **Protection of Privacy Law 5741-1981** ("PPL"), as amended — including by its modernizing amendments that strengthened security-of-data and breach-notification duties — is the principal privacy statute applicable to our processing. The right to privacy is further protected by **Basic Law: Human Dignity and Liberty 5752-1992**.

2.2 The regulator is the **Privacy Protection Authority (PPA)**, which also issues binding guidelines and security standards.

2.3 Supporting statutes include the Penal Law 5737-1977 (unauthorized computer access and child-protection offences) and the Consumer Protection Law 5741-1981.

## 3. Data we collect

3.1 Encre Agent is designed local-first: agent definitions, prompts, responses, chat history, and integrations are stored **on your device** by default.

3.2 Telemetry is off by default. Prompts and responses are not stored server-side by default.

3.3 Optional cloud services (e.g., PiscesLx inference or accounts) process only what is necessary: account identifiers, service metadata, and payment records.

3.4 We do not collect sensitive personal information through ordinary use and never collect minors' data without verifiable parental consent ([MINORS_PRIVACY.md](MINORS_PRIVACY.md)).

## 4. Purposes and legal basis

Processing relies on consent, performance of a contract with you, compliance with legal obligations, and legitimate interests balanced against your rights, consistent with the purposes-limitation and proportionality principles applied in Israeli privacy law.

We do **not** use your prompts, responses, or content to train models without your separate, explicit consent.

## 5. Sharing and international transfers

5.1 We do not sell personal data. Sharing is limited to service providers under contractual safeguards and to authorities where legally compelled.

5.2 Transfers outside Israel rely on adequate protections at the destination or appropriate safeguards, consistent with PPA positions on cross-border transfers; see also our global standards in [DATA_PROCESSING.md](DATA_PROCESSING.md).

## 6. Your rights

Under the PPL you have rights of access to databases holding information about you, correction of inaccurate information, and deletion of outdated or unlawfully held information, plus remedies for privacy infringements. Send requests to dunimd@outlook.com; we respond within approximately 30 days. You may also approach the PPA directly regarding complaints about databases.

## 7. Security and breach response

7.1 We maintain information-security measures aligned with the **Data Security Regulations 5777-2017** baseline expectations: transport encryption (TLS 1.2+), encryption at rest (AES-256-GCM), access controls, event logging, and staff confidentiality undertakings.

7.2 Where a security incident is likely to impair privacy, we notify the **PPA and affected individuals without undue delay**, consistent with the PPL's breach-notification regime, and keep incident records as required.

## 8. Artificial intelligence

8.1 Israel has no comprehensive AI statute; we follow PPA guidance and published national AI policy principles: transparency about machine interaction, fairness, and human oversight.

8.2 AI output may be inaccurate and does not constitute professional advice.

8.3 Synthetic media must be labelled; impersonation is prohibited under the [User Agreement](USER_AGREEMENT.md).
