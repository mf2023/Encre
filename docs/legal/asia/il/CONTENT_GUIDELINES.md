## 2. Prohibited content

Content contrary to Israeli law is prohibited, including:

- Child sexual abuse material (Penal Law 5737-1977 child-protection offences) — zero tolerance; immediate escalation to authorities;
- Incitement to violence or terrorism;
- Defamation, invasion of privacy, and harassment;
- Threats and intimidation;
- Fraud, phishing, and deceptive schemes;
- Intellectual-property infringement (Copyright Law 5708-2007);
- Malware and unauthorized access to systems;
- Content otherwise prohibited by applicable law.

## 3. AI-specific rules

3.1 AI-generated content must be identifiable as such; deepfakes and synthetic media must be labelled.

3.2 Impersonating real persons via AI is prohibited.

3.3 Publishers of AI output are responsible for it.

## 4. Reporting and enforcement

4.1 Report violations to **dunimd@outlook.com** with content and context. CSAM reports are escalated immediately to law enforcement.

4.2 Enforcement ladder: warning → feature restriction → content removal → account termination. Every measure can be appealed.

4.3 We preserve evidence and cooperate with competent Israeli authorities as required by law.

## 5. Changes

Material changes will be announced at least 30 days in advance.
