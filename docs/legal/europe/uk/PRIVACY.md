## 1. Legal basis

This notice is issued under:

- **UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR)**, retained from Regulation (EU) 2016/679
- **Data Protection Act 2018 (DPA 2018)**, including its law-enforcement and intelligence-services regimes
- **Privacy and Electronic Communications (EC Directive) Regulations 2003 (PECR)** for cookies and electronic marketing
- **Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 (DUAA)** — received Royal Assent on 19 June 2025; it amends (but does not replace) the UK GDPR, DPA 2018 and PECR, with changes commenced in stages within 2–12 months of Royal Assent
- Guidance of the **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)**

## 3. Personal data we collect

- **Local-first design**: conversations and settings in Encre Agent remain on your device unless you enable cloud features; we do not collect them (see [Data Processing Rules](DATA_PROCESSING.md)).
- Where collection occurs: account information, billing information, support correspondence, telemetry (**off by default, opt-in only**), and hosted-inference request metadata (timestamps, model IDs, token counts, latency). **Prompts and responses are not stored by default.**
- Special category data is not collected without your explicit consent.

## 4. Purposes of processing

1. Performance of our contract with you — providing and maintaining the services
2. Consent-based processing — optional analytics and telemetry (withdrawable at any time)
3. Legitimate interests (including the DUAA's new "recognised legitimate interests" basis for specified purposes such as crime prevention and safeguarding) — security and abuse prevention
4. Compliance with legal obligations — accounting and tax records

We do not train models on your content without your consent.

## 5. Third parties and international transfers

- We disclose personal data only where a lawful basis applies.
- If you configure a third-party AI provider, your content goes **directly from your device** to that provider.
- International transfers follow the UK GDPR international transfer regime as simplified by the DUAA, using the ICO's International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) or the UK Addendum to EU Standard Contractual Clauses, together with transfer risk assessments.

## 6. Your rights and how to exercise them

Under the UK GDPR and DPA 2018 you may request access (subject access requests), rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection; you may also withdraw consent at any time.

Under the DUAA:

- We conduct **reasonable and proportionate searches** when responding to subject access requests, and apply the "stop-the-clock" rule while awaiting clarification from you;
- Where decisions about you are made solely by automated means with legal or similarly significant effects, we provide information about such decisions, allow you to make representations, and offer human intervention on request;
- We operate a complaints procedure (including an electronic route via email) and tell you the outcome of your complaint.

Send requests to [dunimd@outlook.com](mailto:dunimd@outlook.com). We respond within **one month**, extendable by up to two further months for complex requests. You may complain to us or to the ICO ([ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk)).

## 7. Security measures and breach response

- TLS 1.2+ in transit; AES-256-GCM encryption for local stored data; access control and audit logging
- Personal data breaches are notified to the ICO **without undue delay and within 72 hours** where feasible, and affected individuals are informed where there is a high risk to them

## 8. AI compliance

- The UK regulates AI through its principles-based framework (led by DSIT and sector regulators, with the ICO enforcing data protection in AI contexts); we monitor DUAA implementation and ICO guidance on AI and automated decision-making
- Local mode keeps prompts on your device, structurally avoiding personal data flowing into generative-AI systems
- AI output is probabilistic; verify before professional use

## 9. Changes and contact

Material changes are announced at least **30 days** in advance via [dunimd.com](https://dunimd.com) and in-product notices.
