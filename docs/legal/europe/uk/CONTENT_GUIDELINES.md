## 1. Legal basis

**Online Safety Act 2023** (illegal-content and children's-safety duties); criminal law including the Obscene Publications Act 1959, Terrorism Act 2006, Sexual Offences Act 2003, Public Order Act 1986; the DUAA 2025 changes on storage/access technologies; ICO guidance where processing is involved.

## 2. Prohibited content

Do not create, upload, or generate:

1. Illegal content under the Online Safety Act framework: terrorism, child sexual abuse material (CSAM), incitement to violence, fraud, and other priority illegal content
2. Content harmful to children within the meaning of the Act
3. Non-consensual intimate images or deepfakes of real persons without consent
4. Content inciting hatred or discrimination
5. Malware, phishing, or unlawful surveillance tools

## 3. Obligations regarding AI-generated output

- Label artificially generated media where disclosure would be expected by law or context
- Do not present generated medical, legal, or financial conclusions as professional advice
- Respect copyright when reproducing protected works

## 4. Responsibility by architecture

| Architecture | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Local mode | You are responsible for outputs; nothing reaches us |
| User-configured third-party providers | Requests go directly from your device to that provider; its terms apply |
| Dunimd-hosted features | These guidelines and our abuse procedures apply |

## 5. Enforcement and reporting

Report violations to [dunimd@outlook.com](mailto:dunimd@outlook.com). We assess reports and act against illegal content promptly; suspected CSAM or terrorism content is referred to the appropriate authorities (including the Internet Watch Foundation and police).
