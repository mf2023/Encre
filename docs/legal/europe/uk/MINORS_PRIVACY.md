## 1. Legal basis

UK GDPR Art. 8 and DPA 2018 s.9 (consent age **13** for information-society services); the ICO's **Age Appropriate Design Code (Children's Code)**; the **Online Safety Act 2023** duties regarding children's access to harmful content; and the DUAA 2025 provisions requiring services likely to be accessed by children to consider child protection in design.

## 2. Age categories

| Category | Rule |
|---|---|
| Under 13 | No knowing processing of personal data; accounts deleted on discovery |
| 13–15 | Parental consent where consent is the legal basis |
| 16+ | May consent independently |

## 3. Handling principles

- No behavioural profiling of children for advertising; nudge techniques against children's interests are avoided per the Children's Code
- Local-first design keeps a child's conversations on their device, invisible to us unless cloud features are enabled
- Child-facing notices use plain language

## 4. Rights of persons with parental responsibility

Parents/guardians may exercise access, rectification, erasure, and objection rights for the child via [dunimd@outlook.com](mailto:dunimd@outlook.com), with proportionate identity verification.

## 5. Local-first note

Parents can inspect or erase local data directly on the device because Encre Agent stores data on-device with telemetry off by default.

## 6. Contact

[dunimd@outlook.com](mailto:dunimd@outlook.com). Complaints may also go to the ICO.
