## 1. Legal basis

GDPR Art. 8 (information-society services offered to a child), GDPR Arts. 12, 17, 25 (transparency, erasure, data protection by design), and the **Audiovisual Media Services Directive (EU) 2010/13/EU as amended by Directive (EU) 2018/1808** on the protection of minors.

## 2. Age categories

| Category | Rule |
|---|---|
| Under 13 | We do not knowingly process personal data; accounts are deleted upon discovery |
| 13–15 (or the member-state digital-consent age, which ranges from 13 to 16) | Parental consent required where consent is the legal basis |
| 16+ | May consent independently under GDPR Art. 8 |

Where national law sets the age at 13, 14, or 16, that national threshold applies.

## 3. Handling principles

- No behavioural profiling of children for advertising; no targeted advertising to minors
- Local-first design means conversations stay on the minor's device and are invisible to us unless cloud features are enabled
- Privacy notices addressed to children use plain, clear language

## 4. Rights of holders of parental responsibility

Parents/guardians may exercise access, rectification, erasure, and objection rights on behalf of the child via [dunimd@outlook.com](mailto:dunimd@outlook.com). We verify the request reasonably, balancing verification against further data collection.

## 5. Local-first note

Because Encre Agent stores data on-device with telemetry disabled by default, most processing of a child's data never reaches us — parents can inspect or delete local data directly on the device.

## 6. Contact

Questions about minors' data: [dunimd@outlook.com](mailto:dunimd@outlook.com). You may also contact your national supervisory authority.
