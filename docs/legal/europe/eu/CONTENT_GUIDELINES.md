## 1. Legal basis

- **Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 (Digital Services Act, "DSA")**: illegal-content obligations, notice-and-action mechanisms
- National criminal and civil law of member states (hate speech, defamation, CSAM under **Directive 2011/93/EU**, terrorism content under **Regulation (EU) 2021/784**)
- **AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689)**: prohibited practices (Art. 5) and transparency for AI-generated content

## 2. Prohibited content

Do not create, upload, or generate:

1. Content that is illegal under EU or applicable national law, including terrorist content, child sexual abuse material, and non-consensual intimate imagery
2. Content inciting violence, hatred, or discrimination against protected groups
3. Deepfakes of real persons without consent where the AI Act requires disclosure of artificially generated or manipulated media; political-content deepfakes must be labelled as required by Art. 50 AI Act
4. Content exploiting or endangering minors
5. Malware, phishing, fraud, or unlawful surveillance tools

## 3. Obligations regarding AI-generated output

- Disclose artificial generation where legally required (AI Act Art. 50)
- Do not present generated medical, legal, or financial conclusions as professional advice
- Respect copyright and neighbouring rights when reproducing protected works via prompts

## 4. Responsibility by architecture

| Architecture | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Local mode (on-device models) | You are responsible for outputs; nothing is transmitted to us |
| User-configured third-party providers | Requests go directly from your device to that provider; its terms apply |
| Dunimd-hosted features | These guidelines and our abuse procedures apply |

## 5. Enforcement and reporting

Report violations to [dunimd@outlook.com](mailto:dunimd@outlook.com). We assess notices and act against illegal content consistent with the DSA notice-and-action framework, informing reporters of decisions. Serious criminal conduct should be reported to law enforcement.
