## 1. Legal basis

This notice is issued under:

- **Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, "GDPR")**, applicable since 25 May 2018
- **Directive 2002/58/EC (ePrivacy Directive)** as transposed into national law (cookies and similar technologies)
- **Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act)**, in force since 1 August 2024, with staged application (prohibitions and AI-literacy obligations from 2 February 2025; general-purpose AI and governance rules from 2 August 2025; the remaining provisions apply from 2 August 2026)
- Guidelines of the **European Data Protection Board (EDPB)** and of national supervisory authorities

## 3. Personal data we collect

- **Local-first design**: conversations and settings in Encre Agent and related services are stored on your device. Unless you explicitly enable cloud features, we do not collect these personal data (see [Data Processing Rules](DATA_PROCESSING.md)).
- Where collection occurs: account information, billing information, support correspondence, telemetry (**disabled by default; collected only after you opt in**), and request metadata for hosted inference (timestamps, model identifiers, token counts, latency). **Prompts and responses are not stored by default.**
- Special categories of personal data (GDPR Art. 9) are not collected without your explicit consent.

## 4. Purposes of processing

Processing relies on the legal bases in GDPR Art. 6:

1. Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — providing and maintaining the services, including forwarding requests to AI backends you configure
2. Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — optional analytics and telemetry; you may withdraw consent at any time
3. Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — security, fraud prevention, abuse mitigation
4. Compliance with legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)) — accounting and tax records

We do not use your content to train models unless you have given explicit consent.

## 5. Third parties and international transfers

- We do not disclose personal data to third parties without a lawful basis under GDPR Chapter V.
- If you configure a third-party AI provider, your content is transmitted **directly from your device** to that provider; we do not intermediate such traffic.
- Where we transfer personal data outside the EEA, we rely on an adequacy decision (GDPR Art. 45) or on **Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914**, supplemented by transfer impact assessments.

## 6. Your rights and how to exercise them

Under GDPR Arts. 12–22 you may request:

1. **Access** to your personal data (Art. 15) and information about processing
2. **Rectification** (Art. 16) and **erasure** ("right to be forgotten", Art. 17)
3. **Restriction** of processing (Art. 18) and **data portability** (Art. 20)
4. **Objection** to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21)
5. Withdrawal of consent at any time (Art. 7(3)), without affecting prior processing

Send requests to [dunimd@outlook.com](mailto:dunimd@outlook.com). We respond **within one month**, extendable by two further months for complex requests, and inform you if so. Responses are free of charge in principle. You may also lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority.

## 7. Security measures and breach response

- Encryption in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher; local stored data encrypted with AES-256-GCM; access controls and audit logging
- In case of a personal data breach, we notify the competent supervisory authority **without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours** (GDPR Art. 33), and communicate the breach to affected data subjects when it is likely to result in a high risk (GDPR Art. 34)

## 8. AI and generative-AI compliance

- We monitor the phased application of the **AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689)**. Dunimd does not deploy prohibited practices within the meaning of its Article 5, and assesses classification of its AI features as obligations become applicable.
- In local mode, prompts never pass through our servers, structurally preventing personal data from flowing into generative-AI systems.
- Generative output is probabilistic: always review outputs before professional use.

## 9. Changes and contact

We announce material changes via [dunimd.com](https://dunimd.com) and in-product notices at least **30 days** before they take effect.
