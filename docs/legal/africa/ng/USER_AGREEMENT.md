## 1. Acceptance and Scope

Using any Dunimd service means you accept this agreement and its referenced documents. For Nigerian users this is the sole applicable version.

## 2. Eligibility

2.1 Accounts require a minimum age of 13; users under 18 require verifiable parental consent (see [Minors' Privacy](MINORS_PRIVACY.md)).

## 3. Permitted Use

Run the software locally; build agents and prompts; use open-source components per their licences; connect optional cloud services.

## 4. Prohibited Use

Prohibited:

- unlawful activity under Nigerian law;
- unauthorised access to systems or interference with data (Cybercrimes Act, 2015 as amended);
- cyberstalking and harassment, including section 24 offences;
- fraud, phishing, advance-fee schemes and malware distribution;
- circumventing access controls or overloading services;
- impersonating others or Dunimd.

## 5. Your Content

5.1 You keep all rights in your content; we receive only the limited permission needed to operate the service for you.

5.2 We never train models on your content without express consent ([Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) section 4).

## 6. AI-Generated Output

6.1 Output may be wrong or incomplete; verify important results independently.

6.2 Output is not legal, medical or financial advice.

6.3 Where a person could reasonably believe they are interacting with a human, we disclose the automated nature of the system.

## 7. Suspension, Termination and Changes

7.1 Serious or repeated breaches may result in restriction, suspension or termination; notice and an opportunity to respond are given except where immediate action is required.

7.2 Terminate at any time by deleting your account; local installations continue working.

7.3 Material changes are announced at least 30 days ahead.
