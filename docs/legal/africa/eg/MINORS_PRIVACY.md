## 1. Principles

We minimise children's data, never target children with advertising, and never use children's data for profiling or model training.

## 2. Age Limits and Parental Consent

Cloud-service registration requires a minimum age of 13. For users under 16 we request verifiable parental consent, reflecting the protective spirit of the Child Law No. 12 of 1996 (as amended by Law No. 126 of 2008). If we learn that a child's data was collected without required consent, we delete it promptly.

## 3. Parental Rights

Parents may exercise all rights in the [Privacy Notice](PRIVACY.md) §4 (access, correction, deletion, objection) on behalf of their child via dunimd@outlook.com; we respond within approximately 30 days.

## 4. Local-First Protections

By default, prompts, responses and histories stay on the child's device and are never transmitted; telemetry is opt-in; nothing is used for training without consent.

## 5. Schools and Education

Schools may deploy Encre Agent for teaching. Schools remain responsible for compliance with applicable educational rules; we act as a technical provider only and recommend data-minimisation settings at class level.

## 6. Reporting

Concerns or requests: dunimd@outlook.com · dunimd.com. Content involving child abuse is escalated immediately and reported to competent authorities (see [Content Guidelines](CONTENT_GUIDELINES.md)).
