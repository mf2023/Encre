## 1. Controller and Legal Framework

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Data controller | Dunimd (dunimd.com) |
| Contact | dunimd@outlook.com |
| Services | Encre Agent (local-first AI Agent platform), PiscesLx (hosted LLM inference), StadionOS, Dunimd Enterprise/Cloud/Studio/Support |
| Primary law | Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020 (PDPL) |
| Supervisory authority | Personal Data Protection Center (مركز حماية البيانات الشخصية), Ministry of Communications and Information Technology (MCIT), mcit.gov.eg |

1.1 The PDPL is Egypt's first comprehensive personal-data statute. Its executive regulations have been under development/consultation; we monitor their issuance and will update this notice accordingly.

1.2 Where the PDPL requires licensing or registration of data controllers processing Egyptians' personal data — including foreign controllers operating through a local licensed presence — we will comply through the appropriate mechanism once regulatory processes are operational.

## 2. What We Collect

Encre Agent is designed local-first:

| Data | Where it resides | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Agents, prompts, responses, chat history | Your device only | Never transmitted |
| API keys for third-party providers | Encrypted on your device | Until you delete them |
| Cloud-inference requests (optional) | Processed transiently, then deleted | Off unless enabled |
| Service metadata (timestamps, error codes) | Server-side | Max. 30 days |
| Telemetry | Server-side | Opt-in only; off by default |
| Account data (cloud services only) | Server-side | Until account deletion |

2.2 We do not store your prompts or model responses by default, and we do not use your content to train models without your explicit consent.

## 3. Purposes and Legal Bases

We process personal data to: provide and operate the services you request (contract); secure our systems and prevent abuse (legitimate interests/legal obligation); respond to support requests (contract); comply with Egyptian legal requirements (legal obligation).

Sensitive data (as defined by the PDPL) receives heightened protection and is processed only with explicit consent or another lawful basis permitted by law.

## 4. Your Rights

Subject to the PDPL and its implementing framework, you may request:

1. Access to your personal data;
2. Correction of inaccurate data;
3. Deletion of data;
4. Objection to processing;
5. Withdrawal of consent where processing is consent-based;
6. Information about processing purposes and recipients.

Requests go to dunimd@outlook.com. We aim to respond within approximately 30 days, subject to statutory timelines under the PDPL and its executive regulations.

## 5. Cross-Border Transfers

Transfers of personal data outside Egypt are subject to PDPL restrictions, including approval requirements administered by the competent authority. Our architecture minimises such transfers: core processing occurs on your device in Egypt. Where cloud services involve international infrastructure, we apply contractual safeguards and will follow authority-approval procedures as they become operational.

## 6. Security and Incident Response

Technical measures: TLS 1.2+ for transport, AES-256-GCM at rest, least-privilege access. Organisational measures: confidentiality undertakings, logging, periodic reviews.

If a personal-data breach occurs, we notify affected individuals and the competent authority without undue delay, in accordance with the PDPL (and any timelines set by its executive regulations once issued).

## 7. Artificial Intelligence

Egypt has a national AI strategy and a National Council for Artificial Intelligence under MCIT, but no comprehensive AI statute; data-protection law and general law apply to AI systems. Accordingly we commit to:

- Transparency that you are interacting with AI systems;
- No training on user content without explicit consent;
- Human review available for consequential automated decisions affecting you.
