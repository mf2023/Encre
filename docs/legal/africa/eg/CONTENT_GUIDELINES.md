## 2. Prohibited Content

The following may not be shared via shared or cloud services:

- Child sexual abuse material — criminal under Egyptian law (Penal Code provisions on debauchery/exploitation of children, incl. Art. 178 bis Penal Code as applicable); escalated immediately to authorities;
- Content violating Cybercrime Law No. 175 of 2018 (hacking, privacy invasion, incitement);
- Terrorism-related content;
- Defamation and serious infringement of personal rights;
- Copyright or trademark infringements (Copyright Law No. 82 of 2002);
- Phishing, fraud or malware;
- Content otherwise contrary to mandatory Egyptian law or public morals as codified in law.

## 3. AI-Specific Rules

AI-generated content must not be passed off as human-created; synthetic media (deepfakes) must be labelled. Impersonation of real persons is prohibited. Egypt's national AI strategy emphasises responsible AI; we align with its transparency principles even absent a comprehensive statute.

## 4. Reporting and Enforcement

Report violations to dunimd@outlook.com (include content and context). CSAM reports trigger immediate escalation. Enforcement ladder: warning → feature restriction → content removal → account ban; every measure can be appealed.
