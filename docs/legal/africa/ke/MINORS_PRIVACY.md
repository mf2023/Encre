## 1. Principles

Section 33 of the Data Protection Act, 2019 requires consideration of the child's best interests and verifiable parental consent for processing a child's data. We apply it strictly: minimal collection, no advertising profiles, no sale of children's data.

## 2. Age Limits and Parental Consent

2.1 Cloud accounts require a minimum age of 13. Users under 18 need verifiable parental consent before activation.

2.2 Local basic functions require no account; the same expectations apply.

2.3 Data held without required consent is deleted promptly upon discovery.

## 3. Parents' Rights

Parents and guardians may exercise section 26 rights on a child's behalf: access, correction, deletion, objection. Write to dunimd@outlook.com; we respond within approximately 30 days.

## 4. Local-First Architecture as Protection

Prompts, responses and history stay on the device by default; telemetry is off by default; nothing about a minor's usage leaves the machine without explicit opt-in; no training on minors' content without express consent.

## 5. Schools and Non-Commercial Use

Schools may deploy Encre Agent for teaching. We act strictly on documented school instructions and encourage data-minimisation settings at class level.

## 6. Contact and Reporting

6.1 Contact: dunimd@outlook.com · dunimd.com

6.2 Suspected child exploitation material must be reported immediately to dunimd@outlook.com and to law enforcement; we escalate without delay.

Regulator: Office of the Data Protection Commissioner, odpc.go.ke.
