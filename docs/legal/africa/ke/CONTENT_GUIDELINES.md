## 1. Purpose and Architecture

Encre Agent processes agents locally by default; these guidelines apply when you publish, share or connect content to optional services.

| Layer | Handling | Guidelines |
|---|---|---|
| Local agent on device | no server inspection | your responsibility |
| Shared/published agents | moderated | fully applicable |
| Cloud inference (PiscesLx) | technical processing | abuse prevention |
| Chat-platform integrations | platform rules parallel | follow those policies too |

## 2. Prohibited Content

Prohibited via shared or cloud surfaces:

- **child sexual abuse material** — immediate escalation to law enforcement; prohibited under the Children Act, 2022 and the Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act, 2018;
- cyber harassment, false publications and hate speech contrary to the Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act, 2018;
- incitement to violence or discrimination;
- credible threats, stalking and doxxing;
- infringement of copyright (Copyright Act, as amended 2019, including notice-and-takedown procedures);
- fraud, phishing and malware distribution;
- other content prohibited by Kenyan law.

## 3. AI-Specific Rules

3.1 AI-generated media must be identifiable as synthetic where a reasonable person could be misled; undisclosed deepfakes targeting real people are prohibited.

3.2 Impersonation of individuals or of Dunimd is not allowed. Users who publish AI output are responsible for it.

## 4. Reporting and Enforcement

4.1 Report violations to **dunimd@outlook.com** with relevant content and context; CSAM reports trigger immediate escalation.

4.2 Escalating measures: warning → feature restriction → removal → suspension. Every measure can be appealed by replying to the enforcement notice.

4.3 We may refer criminal conduct to the Directorate of Criminal Investigations or other competent authorities.

## 5. Changes

Changes to these guidelines are published at least 30 days before taking effect.
