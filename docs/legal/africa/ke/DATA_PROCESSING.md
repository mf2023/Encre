## 1. Local-First Declaration

Agent definitions, prompts, responses and history remain on your device by default; telemetry is opt-in only; prompts and responses are not stored server-side unless a cloud feature requires it.

## 2. Data Flows

| Data | Location | Retention |
|---|---|---|
| Agent configs, workflows | device only | until deletion |
| Conversation history | device only | user-controlled |
| API keys (third-party providers) | encrypted on device | until deletion |
| Cloud inference requests | deleted after processing | session |
| Service metadata (timestamps, error codes) | server-side | max 30 days |
| Telemetry (opt-in only) | server-side | max 90 days |
| Account data (cloud only) | server-side | until account deletion, plus legal holds |

## 3. Processing by Service

3.1 **PiscesLx:** TLS-encrypted requests processed in memory; metadata purged after 30 days automatically.

3.2 **Encre Agent Desktop/Framework:** no server-side processing.

3.3 **Chat-platform integrations:** platform policies govern platform-side data.

3.4 **Enterprise:** processing under a written processor agreement with the customer.

## 4. Telemetry

Opt-in only; disable at any time in settings — existing records are deleted on disable.

## 5. International Transfers

Transfers outside Kenya rely on appropriate safeguards consistent with sections 48–49 DPA (contractual commitments aligned with recognised international frameworks such as standard contractual clauses) or statutory exceptions including consent.

## 6. Security and Breach Response

6.1 Technical: TLS 1.2+, AES-256-GCM at rest, least-privilege access. Organisational: confidentiality undertakings, access logging, periodic reviews.

6.2 **Breach procedure:** notify the ODPC within **72 hours** where a breach is likely to result in risk to data subjects (section 43), inform affected individuals without undue delay, and maintain internal incident records for review.

## 7. Changes and Contact

Material changes are published at least 30 days before they take effect. Contact: dunimd@outlook.com · dunimd.com · Regulator: ODPC, odpc.go.ke.
