## 2. Legal framework and regulator

| Instrument | Relevance |
|---|---|
| **POPIA — Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013** | General data protection; fully in force since 1 July 2021 |
| **PAIA — Promotion of Access to Information Act 2 of 2000** | Access-to-information requests |
| **Cybercrimes Act 19 of 2020** | Unlawful access, data interference, harmful messages |
| **ECTA 25 of 2002** | Electronic transactions and consumer communications |
| **Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008** | Consumer rights incl. direct marketing opt-in |

Regulator: **Information Regulator (South Africa)** — inforegulator.org.za. Complaints may be lodged with the Regulator after raising them with us.

## 3. What we collect

Encre Agent is local-first: agent definitions, prompts, responses and history stay on your device. Telemetry is off by default; prompts/responses are not stored by default. Cloud services (optional) process account details, service metadata (up to 30 days) and opt-in telemetry (up to 90 days).

## 4. Why we process (justification)

POPIA s.11 grounds used: your consent (opt-in features), performance of a contract (cloud services), legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention), legal obligations. We do not use your content to train models without explicit consent.

## 5. Sharing and cross-border transfers

5.1 We do not sell personal information.

5.2 Recipients: hosting providers, payment processors — bound by written operator agreements (POPIA s.20–21).

5.3 Transfers abroad follow POPIA s.72 conditions (adequate protection, binding rules, consent or necessity); prior-authorisation scenarios under s.58 are respected.

## 6. Your rights

Under POPIA ss.23–25 and PAIA you may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal information, object to processing, and withdraw consent. We respond within a reasonable time (customarily within 30 days). Requests: dunimd@outlook.com. If unsatisfied, you may complain to the Information Regulator.

## 7. Children

We do not knowingly process children's personal information (POPIA s.35 prohibits it absent limited grounds). See [Minors' Privacy](MINORS_PRIVACY.md).

## 8. Security and breach notification

Technical measures include TLS 1.2+ transport encryption and AES-256-GCM storage encryption (s.19 safeguards). Where a breach is likely to result in harm, both the **Information Regulator** and affected individuals are notified as soon as reasonably possible after discovery (s.22).

## 9. AI and generative AI

9.1 We disclose when you interact with automated systems; generated outputs are labelled where feasible.

9.2 No model training on user content without consent; no fully automated decisions producing legal effects without human review on request.

9.3 South Africa has no comprehensive AI statute as of this update; POPIA, Cybercrimes Act and common-law duties apply. We monitor guidance from the Information Regulator.

## 10. Changes and contact

Material changes are announced at least 30 days in advance. Contact: dunimd@outlook.com.
