## 1. Principles

Minimal collection; no behavioural advertising to children; no sale of children's data; POPIA s.35 prohibition on processing children's information is respected (limited statutory exceptions aside).

## 2. Age limits and consent

2.1 Cloud registration requires age 13+. Users aged 13–17 require verifiable parental/guardian consent — a prudent standard given POPIA's strict child-data rules (Children's Act 38 of 2005 governs capacity).

2.2 Local core features need no account but remain subject to the same age floor.

2.3 Data of minors registered without required consent is deleted on discovery.

## 3. Parents' rights

Parents may exercise all [Privacy Notice](PRIVACY.md) §6 rights for their child: access, correction, deletion, objection, withdrawal. Requests via dunimd@outlook.com are answered within about 30 days with identity verification.

## 4. Local-first as protection

Prompts, responses and history stay on-device; telemetry off by default; no cloud storage without activation; never used for model training without explicit consent.

## 5. Schools and non-commercial use

Schools may deploy Encre Agent for teaching; class-level data minimisation recommended; schools remain responsible for compliance with education-sector rules and their own policies.

## 6. Contact and escalation

dunimd@outlook.com · dunimd.com · Regulator: Information Regulator (South Africa). Child-safety concerns online: Film and Publication Board / SAPS.
