## 2. Prohibited content

The following must not be shared via shared or cloud surfaces:

- **Child sexual abuse material** — strictly prohibited (Films and Publications Act 65 of 1996 as amended, ss.26–27; Criminal Law (Sexual Offences) Act 32 of 2007); escalated immediately;
- **Hate speech and harmful messages** inciting violence or discrimination (Cybercrimes Act 19 of 2020 ss.14–16; PEPUDA 4 of 2000);
- incitement of property damage (Cybercrimes Act s.14);
- defamation and false statements harming reputation;
- intimate images without consent;
- malware, phishing, fraud (Cybercrimes Act s.13; common law);
- copyright/trademark infringement;
- other unlawful content under South African law.

## 3. AI-specific rules

Synthetic media ("deepfakes") must be labelled; AI content must not be passed off as human-created where this would deceive; users remain responsible for published AI output. No comprehensive AI statute exists yet in South Africa — these rules apply voluntarily and reflect international practice.

## 4. Reporting and enforcement

4.1 Report violations to **dunimd@outlook.com** with context. CSAM reports are actioned immediately and escalated to authorities (Film and Publication Board takedown channels).

4.2 Ladder: warning → feature limits → removal → suspension. Every measure can be appealed by reply email.

4.3 We reserve statutory reporting duties to law enforcement.

## 5. Changes

Changes are announced at least 30 days ahead.
