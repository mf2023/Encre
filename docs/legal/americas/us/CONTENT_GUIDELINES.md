## 1. Architecture and Applicability

1.1 Encre Agent is local-first: locally stored agents and prompts involve only you, and these guidelines apply when content is published, shared, or processed through optional services.

| Layer | Processing | Guidelines apply |
|---|---|---|
| Local agents on device | No server processing | Your responsibility |
| Shared/published agents | Reviewed | Fully |
| Cloud inference (PiscesLx) | Technical processing | Abuse prevention |
| Chat-platform integrations | Platform rules also apply | Follow platform policies |

1.2 For user-generated content we generally function as an interactive computer service under 47 U.S.C. § 230; for content we ourselves generate (AI outputs) we act as a content creator subject to ordinary legal standards.

## 2. Prohibited Content

Prohibited via shared or cloud services:

- **Child sexual abuse material (CSAM):** strictly prohibited (18 U.S.C. §§ 2251 et seq.; TAKE IT DOWN Act for non-consensual intimate imagery of minors); reports go to NCMEC and law enforcement — no exceptions;
- Non-consensual intimate imagery of any person;
- Terrorist or violent extremist content and solicitation;
- Credible threats, stalking, targeted harassment, doxxing;
- Fraud, phishing, malware distribution, or violations of the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (18 U.S.C. § 1030);
- Infringement of copyrights, trademarks, or other IP (DMCA process in [Terms](TERMS.md) §6);
- Content facilitating self-harm where exploitative, and other content violating applicable law.

## 3. AI-Specific Rules

3.1 **Transparency:** synthetic media and AI-generated content must not be passed off as authentic human-created content where that would deceive; several state laws (e.g., Utah AI Policy Act; Texas TRAIGA effective January 1, 2026; Colorado AI Act since June 30, 2026) impose disclosure duties on consumer-facing AI interactions, which our integrations implement.

3.2 **No impersonation:** do not use AI to impersonate real people, officials, or brands deceptively.

3.3 **Responsibility:** publishers of AI output own its consequences.

## 4. Reporting and Enforcement

4.1 Report violations to **dunimd@outlook.com** with context; suspected CSAM triggers immediate escalation to NCMEC (report.cybertip.org).

4.2 Enforcement tiers: warning → feature restriction → content removal → account suspension/termination. Every measure may be appealed by replying to the notice.

4.3 We preserve evidence and cooperate with lawful law-enforcement requests per applicable process.

## 5. Changes

Changes to these guidelines are announced at least 30 days before taking effect.
