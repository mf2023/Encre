## 1. Principles

1.1 We collect the minimum necessary from minors and never serve targeted advertising to them.

1.2 Our services are general-purpose; they are not directed to children under 13.

## 2. Children Under 13 (COPPA)

2.1 Under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act and Rule (as amended April 2025, full compliance required from 2026), we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 absent verifiable parental consent.

2.2 Where consent applies, we provide: direct notice of practices; obtain verifiable parental consent (using methods matched to risk, including generic confirmation plus additional verification); separate parental consent before any third-party disclosure beyond supporting providers; and honor parental revocation and deletion requests.

2.3 We maintain reasonable data security for children's data per our written security program and retain it only as long as necessary.

2.4 If we learn we hold a child's data without valid consent, we delete it promptly.

## 3. Teens 13–17 Under State Laws

3.1 California and several states prohibit selling or sharing personal information of consumers under 16 without affirmative authorization ("opt-in"); we do not sell or share teens' information at all.

3.2 Teens with statutory capacity may exercise privacy rights directly; otherwise parents exercise them on their behalf.

3.3 California's Age-Appropriate Design Code remains enjoined in litigation; we voluntarily apply high-privacy defaults for teen accounts regardless.

## 4. Parental Rights and Requests

4.1 Parents may review, delete, refuse further collection of, or correct a child's information by emailing dunimd@outlook.com from the address associated with the account; we verify parental identity as appropriate and respond within COPPA/state timelines.

4.2 Complaints: FTC (reportfraud.ftc.gov) or your state attorney general.

## 5. Local-First Protections

5.1 Encre Agent keeps prompts, responses, and history on-device by default; nothing about a minor's usage reaches us unless optional cloud features are enabled.

5.2 Telemetry is opt-in. We do not train models on user content without explicit consent.

## 6. Schools and Contact

6.1 When Encre Agent is deployed by an educational institution, student records may be protected under FERPA (20 U.S.C. § 1232g) and state student-privacy laws; in that case the institution controls the data and directs requests.

6.2 Contact: dunimd@outlook.com · dunimd.com.
