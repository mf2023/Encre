## 1. Core principles

1.1 We collect the minimum data necessary from minors and never profile them for marketing.

1.2 Our services are general-audience products; we do not knowingly target children.

## 2. Age thresholds and consent

2.1 Canadian privacy statutes set no fixed self-registration age; applying a prudent standard, accounts require **age 13+**, and users **under 16** need verifiable parental/guardian consent before cloud features are activated.

2.2 Local core functions need no account but remain subject to the same age floor.

2.3 If we learn a minor's data exists without required consent, we delete it.

## 3. Parental rights

3.1 Parents may exercise all access/correction/deletion rights under PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25 on behalf of their children:

- request access to collected data;
- request correction or deletion;
- withdraw consent for further processing.

3.2 Requests: dunimd@outlook.com — answered within ~30 days with identity verification steps appropriate to protecting the child.

## 4. Local-first protection

4.1 Prompts, responses, and history stay on-device; nothing transmits without opt-in.

4.2 Telemetry defaults off; no model training on minor content, ever, absent explicit parental consent.

4.3 No behavioural advertising to minors through any Dunimd surface.

## 5. Schools and educational use

5.1 Schools may deploy Encre Agent for instruction; we recommend class-level minimization settings and no personal data entry into shared agents.

5.2 Where provincial education acts or school-board policies govern student records, those frameworks prevail; we act as a processor only.
